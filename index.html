    Shipping & Returns

    What to expect once you place an order.

    Shipping

    • Free delivery on all orders over $50; a flat $4.99 fee applies below that.
    • Standard delivery: 3–5 business days.
    • Express delivery available at checkout in select areas.
    • You can track any order's status from your Order History page.

    Returns & Refunds

    • Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery, in original condition.
    • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the return.
    • Damaged or incorrect items are covered at no extra cost — contact support.

    Need to start a return?

    Reach out through Customer Support with your order ID and we'll walk you through it.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 2026

    What We Collect

    When you create an account, place an order, or contact support, we collect your name, email, phone number, shipping address, and order history. Payment details are handled by our payment processor — we never store raw card numbers.

    How We Use It

    • To process and deliver your orders.
    • To send order updates and respond to support requests.
    • To improve our product catalog and recommendations.
    • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

    Your Choices

    You can view and update your details anytime from your Profile page, and you can contact Customer Support to request deletion of your account and associated data.

    Help Center

    Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact us.

    Tap the person icon in the header, then "Create one" on the login screen. Fill in your name, email, and a password — you're shopping in under a minute. You can also try the demo accounts on that page without registering.

    Go to your Profile page (the person icon, or "Track an Order" in the footer) and open the Order History tab. Every past order and its current status is listed there.

    Tap the heart icon on any product card, or the heart icon in the header to view your saved list. Items stay saved so you can add them to your cart whenever you're ready.

    Tap "Compare" on up to 4 product cards, then open the compare icon (the ⇄ arrows) in the header to see them side-by-side — price, rating, stock, and specs all lined up together.

    Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Delivery. You'll choose your method at checkout — see the full breakdown on our Payment Methods page.

    On your Cart page, enter your code in the "Coupon code" box and tap Apply. The discount updates your order summary immediately. Try KENE10 for 10% off.

    Contact Customer Support with your order ID as soon as possible. We can usually adjust or cancel an order before it ships; once it's out for delivery, you'll need to use our returns process instead.

    Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery in original condition, with refunds issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Full details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
